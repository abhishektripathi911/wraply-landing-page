The problem

Closeout is still an afterthought, until it is a schedule risk.

Most project teams juggle spreadsheets, email threads, and ad hoc trackers. By the time the job is nearing substantial completion, the closeout package is already behind and the scramble at the end can strain the relationship with the owner when the project team does not finish strong at closeout.

Weeks lost to manual tracking

PMs and project engineers spend hours building registers, chasing subs, and updating status columns that go out of date as soon as the next email lands.

Documents scattered everywhere

Warranties and O&M manuals live across email, Procore, SharePoint, and shared drives. No single source of truth for what is required versus what has actually been received.

Owners get incomplete handover

Operations and facilities teams inherit binders that are hard to search, missing key documents, and out of sync with the final as builds and submittals.

The Wraply.AI solution

Turn your specs, submittals, and RFIs into a live closeout engine.

Wraply.AI reads your contract specs, submittals, and project docs to auto build a complete closeout requirement matrix, track progress in real time, and output a digital, searchable turnover package. Commissioning workflows are part of our future roadmap.

  • AI closeout matrix. Automatically maps required warranties, O&M manuals, test reports, training, and spare parts from your specs and submittals.
  • Automated sub workflows. Routes requests to each subcontractor, tracks responses, and highlights what is missing or non compliant.
  • Digital turnover binder. Outputs a structured, searchable closeout package that aligns with owner requirements and future facilities use.
  • Fits your existing stack. Works alongside Procore, CMiC, SharePoint, and your current document management workflows.
Example
From messy documents to an organized closeout matrix.

Wraply.AI takes messy, unstructured documents and continuously routes them into the right “folders” by spec, division, trade, and requirement status—so by the time you reach substantial completion, your closeout matrix is already organized instead of being a last-minute scramble.
