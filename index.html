Closeout is a scramble that hurts owner trust.

Spreadsheets, emails, and outdated trackers create chaos. By substantial completion, teams are already behind — and the last-minute scramble often strains the relationship with the owner when the project team doesn’t finish strong.

Manual tracking eats weeks

PMs and PEs chase subs, update trackers, and rebuild status columns that immediately go stale.

Documents scattered everywhere

Warranties, O&M, and test reports live in email, Procore, SharePoint, and drives — no single source of truth.

Owners receive incomplete handover

Missing documents and disorganized binders frustrate operations teams and impact turnover quality.

The Wraply.AI solution

Turn specs, submittals, and RFIs into a live closeout engine.

Wraply.AI reads your contract documents to auto-build a complete closeout requirement matrix, track progress in real-time, and output a digital, searchable turnover package. Commissioning workflows are on our future roadmap.

  • AI closeout matrix. Maps required warranties, O&M manuals, test reports, training, and attic stock from your specs and submittals.
  • Automated sub workflows. Routes requests to each subcontractor, tracks responses, and highlights what is missing or non-compliant.
  • Digital turnover binder. Outputs a structured, searchable closeout package aligned with owner requirements and facilities use.
  • Fits your stack. Works alongside Procore, CMiC, SharePoint, and your existing document workflows.
